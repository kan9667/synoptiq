DATA CORPUS

10-Year Historical Corpus & Benchmark Replay

Full 10-Year Chronological Corpus (2010–2019)

The Synoptiq dataset pipeline processes 10 years of aligned GEFSv12 forecasts and IMD daily observations:

TRAINING SPLIT 2010–2015 1,281,735 eligible rows Threshold q90 & LightGBM fitting
VALIDATION SPLIT 2016–2017 427,635 eligible rows Platt sigmoid calibration fitting
HELD-OUT TEST SPLIT 2018–2019 427,050 eligible rows Brier score & reliability evaluation

Why 3 Frozen Dates in the Interactive Dashboard?

To guarantee instant, zero-latency offline performance and avoid multi-gigabyte memory overhead during demonstrations, the web dashboard bundles three frozen benchmark test dates (2018-01-01, 2019-01-01, 2019-12-31). The full corpus remains archived in data/processed/rows.parquet for end-to-end retraining.

Frozen chronological split policy defined in config/splits.yaml
